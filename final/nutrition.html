Fuel & Mindset

Nutrition basics that stay simple.

You don't need a strict diet to eat well. These principles focus on consistency over perfection.

Principle

Build Balanced Plates

Aim for a mix of protein, vegetables, and whole grains at most meals rather than counting every gram.

Principle

Hydrate First

Thirst is often mistaken for hunger. Start with water before reaching for a snack.

Principle

Plan Simple Meals

A short list of go-to meals reduces decision fatigue during busy weeks.

Quick Meal Ideas

Easy plates to start with

Breakfast

Greek yogurt, berries, and a handful of oats or granola.

Lunch

Grilled chicken or beans over greens with olive oil and lemon.

Dinner

Baked salmon or tofu, roasted vegetables, and a small portion of rice.

Stay Consistent

Set one small, specific goal each week instead of overhauling everything at once.

Track Progress Kindly

Notice energy, sleep, and mood — not just the scale — as signs of progress.

Celebrate Small Wins

A completed workout or a balanced meal is a win worth recognizing on its own.
